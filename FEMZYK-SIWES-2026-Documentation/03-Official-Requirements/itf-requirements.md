## Industrial Training Fund (ITF) — SIWES requirements applied

The Industrial Training Fund (ITF) administers SIWES in Nigeria. The following
requirements were applied to this documentation package:

### Start of SIWES

- **SCAF (Students Commencement of Attachment Form)** — completed, signed and stamped by
  4000 YEARS LTD and submitted, together with the organization's acceptance letter, to
  the ITF area office closest to the student's place of attachment at the start of the
  placement.

### During SIWES

- Log Book kept daily; entries dated, personalized and written in the past tense.
- Entries record what the student personally did, learned, practised, observed, assisted
  with, developed, tested, corrected or documented.
- Grammar, spelling, punctuation and technical accuracy checked.
- Diagrams / pictures included in the Log Book (9 total, exceeding the ITF minimum of 4).

### End of SIWES

- **ITF Form 8** duly completed, signed by the industry-based supervisor (Ifeoluwa
  Wole-Osho), stamped with the organization's stamp (4000 YEARS LTD), and submitted to
  the school together with the two signed/stamped Log Books.

## Honesty rules applied

- Signatures, stamps and supervisor comments are never typed or simulated.
- Attendance is recorded honestly.
- No fabricated activities, dates, technologies, evidence or approvals.
- Pre-existing organizational code is not claimed as the student's work.
