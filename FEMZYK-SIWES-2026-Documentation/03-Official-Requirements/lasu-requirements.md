## Lagos State University (LASU) — SIWES requirements for 400-Level Computer Science

The following requirements, as provided by the department/SIWES office, were applied in
this documentation package:

1. **Host organization must be Computer Science related.** Satisfied by 4000 YEARS LTD
   (software development / digital technology services).
2. **Duration:** approximately six (6) months. Satisfied by the 28-week placement
   (2 March 2026 – 12 September 2026).
3. **SIWES letter copy** submitted to the HOD's office.
4. **Log Book** obtained from the SIWES office.
5. **Two physical Log Books** used — covering Weeks 1–14 and Weeks 15–28 (28 weeks total).
6. **SCAF** signed and stamped by the organization and submitted with the acceptance
   letter to the ITF office closest to the student's place of attachment at the start of
   SIWES.
7. SIWES requirements obtained from the relevant ITF office.
8. **At least 5 diagrams / pictures** in the Log Book. Satisfied by 9 diagrams completed
   across the 28 weeks.
9. At the end of SIWES, **ITF Form 8 + Log Book**, duly signed and stamped by the
   industry-based supervisor and the organization, submitted to the school.
10. By the end of SIWES the student must demonstrate capability to build a **full-scale
    software application that solves a real problem and includes a database.** Satisfied
    by the KLC Exam Portal (Java 17 / JavaFX / Supabase PostgreSQL + H2) documented in
    Weeks 25–28.
