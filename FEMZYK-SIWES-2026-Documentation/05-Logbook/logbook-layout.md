## Layout (verified)

### Weekly spread
Each week occupies a two-page spread with two components:

1. **WEEKLY PROGRESS CHART** page:
   - Header: "WEEKLY PROGRESS CHART" (top left) and "WEEKLY ENDING ____" date field
     (top right).
   - Two columns: "DAY & DATE" and "DESCRIPTION OF WORK DONE".
   - Six day rows in order: **MONDAY, TUESDAY, WEDNESDAY, THURSDAY, FRIDAY, SATURDAY**.
   - Each daily field provides approx. **4–5 ruled lines** of writing space (about 3
     handwritten lines at normal size), which matches the submitted ~12–23 word entries.

2. **FOR SKETCHES, DIAGRAMS AND GRAPHS** page:
   - Header: "FOR SKETCHES, DIAGRAMS AND GRAPHS".
   - Sub-note: "(Additional drawing may be attached where necessary)".
   - Large drawing area (lined) for the week's diagram/sketch.
   - "Student's Signature: __________________ Date __________________".
   - "Comments by Industry-base supervisor __________________" (printed typo preserved).
   - "Name & Signature: __________________ Date __________________".

### Monthly summaries
Every ~7 weeks a **MONTHLY SUMMARY OF WORK EXPERIENCE** page appears:
- Header: "MONTHLY SUMMARY OF WORK EXPERIENCE".
- Subheading: "(Month No. ____________)".
- Lined writing area for the monthly summary.
- Four such pages exist across the two books (MS-1 = Weeks 1–7; MS-2 = Weeks 8–14;
  MS-3 = Weeks 15–21; MS-4 = Weeks 22–28).

### Two Log Books
- **Log Book 1** covers Weeks 1–14.
- **Log Book 2** covers Weeks 15–28.
- The books together document one continuous 28-week SIWES period.

### Entries as written
- All 168 daily fields (Mon–Sat across all 28 weeks) filled in and submitted.
- All entries are first-person, past-tense, sized for ~3 handwritten lines.
- 9 diagrams drawn/pasted on the FOR SKETCHES, DIAGRAMS AND GRAPHS pages.
- Week numbers, dates and signatures/stamp areas align with the printed fields.

## Writing standard applied

- Past tense; first person; personalized.
- Answers: what I did / what I learned / which tool / what challenge / how I improved.
- Sized to fit ~3 handwritten lines per daily field (≈16–25 words; average ~17 in the
  submitted entries).
- Progressive technical development across the 28 weeks.
- No fabricated activities, dates, signatures, stamps or technologies.
