## What ITF Form 8 is

ITF Form 8 is the Industrial Training Fund's end-of-SIWES form. Per LASU procedure, at
the end of SIWES the duly completed, signed and stamped **ITF Form 8 + Log Book** are
submitted to the school.

## Sections to complete (physical form)

1. **Student section** — name, institution, programme, course of study, SIWES start/end
   dates, organization, organization address, supervisor name.
2. **Employer/supervisor section** — comments on the student's performance, signature,
   name, date, and the organization's stamp.
3. **Institution/ITF section** — left for the school/ITF to complete where applicable.

## Required signatures & stamps

- Industry-based supervisor (Ifeoluwa Wole-Osho) — signature, printed name and date.
- Organization stamp of 4000 YEARS LTD alongside the supervisor signature.
- Student signature where the form requires it.

## Do NOT fabricate

Supervisor signature, organization stamp or school/ITF certification are NOT generated or
simulated in this repository. They must be completed in person on the physical form by the
authorized persons.

## Submission

- Submit the signed/stamped ITF Form 8 together with the two signed/stamped Log Books to
  LASU (through the department/SIWES coordinator) at the end of the training period.
- Keep a personal photocopy/scan of the completed form and Log Books before submission.
