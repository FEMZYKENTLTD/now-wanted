## Rule

Wherever the industry supervisor signs, the organization stamp (4000 YEARS LTD) must be
placed alongside the signature. This applies to the weekly FOR SKETCHES, DIAGRAMS AND
GRAPHS pages, the MONTHLY SUMMARY pages (where the book instructs), and ITF Form 8.

## Confirmed weekly sign-off fields

Each week's FOR SKETCHES, DIAGRAMS AND GRAPHS page ends with:

1. **Student's Signature: __________________ Date __________________**
2. **Comments by Industry-base supervisor __________________** (printed typo preserved)
3. **Name & Signature: __________________ Date __________________**

## Placeholder convention (physical forms only)

Signature, stamp and date fields are left blank in the digital source and completed in
ink on the physical form by the appropriate person. Nothing is typed, drawn or simulated
to look like a real signature or stamp.
