## What SCAF is

SCAF is the SIWES commencement form completed at the **start** of training. It confirms
that the student has begun their industrial attachment at the host organization and is
signed/stamped by the organization before being sent to ITF.

## Status

- Completed, signed and stamped by 4000 YEARS LTD.
- Submitted with the acceptance letter to the ITF area office at the start of SIWES
  (March 2026).
